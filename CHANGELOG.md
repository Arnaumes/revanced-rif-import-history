# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-11

First release.

- **Import history**: adds **Import history** to rif's **Settings → Backup**. Pick a backup made with
  **Export settings AND HISTORY** and its history is added to rif's history. Items already in your history are
  skipped and left unchanged, and nothing is removed.

Requires rif is fun **5.6.22** (free or golden platinum). Patch your original APK together with the patches you
already use, such as the official **Spoof client** patch,
[MojiRS/revanced-rif-patches](https://github.com/MojiRS/revanced-rif-patches) and
[notBrian2/revanced-rif-fixes](https://github.com/notBrian2/revanced-rif-fixes).
